# 🦇 Project Codename: **NIGHTBOUND** (placeholder)
### Game Design Reference Document v0.1

---

## 1. Core Identity

| Aspect | Direction |
|--------|-----------|
| **Genre** | 3D Horde Survival (Vampire Survivors-like) |
| **Perspective** | Third-person, similar to MegaBonk |
| **Aesthetic** | Gothic Horror × 70s Retrofuturism |
| **Tone** | Dark but stylish, not campy (think Castlevania meets Blade) |

### Differentiator from Vampire Survivors
> **You ARE the monster.** Play as vampires, werewolves, and mythological creatures defending against hunters, cultists, and rival monsters. Inverts the formula while keeping the theme.

## 2. Core Mechanics Checklist

- [ ] **Weapon Fusion System** — Combine two weapons into hybrid forms
- [ ] **Terrain Interactions** — Ice (slide), lava (DOT kiting), water (slow + conductivity)
- [ ] **Verticality** — Jumping, air attacks, multi-level arenas
- [ ] **Momentum Combat** — Speed = damage multiplier
